# Fleet Battler particle atlas

`SpaceFx.png` is a 512 × 512 RGBA atlas with 128 × 128 cells.

- Cells 0–8 use the nine explosion frames arranged by TheJosh from Kenney's Smoke Particles. [Explosion particles sprite atlas](https://opengameart.org/content/explosion-particles-sprite-atlas)
- Cells 9–11 use Kenney's `light_01.png`, `spark_01.png`, and `flame_01.png`. [Kenney Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack) · [Download mirror](https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-particle-pack)

Both sources release these images under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The sample resizes and repacks the source images.
